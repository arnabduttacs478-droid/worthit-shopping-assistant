# WorthIt? 🛍️

### Spend smarter. Buy what actually fits your needs.

**WorthIt?** is an open-source, AI-powered shopping decision assistant designed to help people make more informed purchasing decisions. Instead of choosing products based solely on price, popularity, or ratings, users can compare options according to their budget, intended use, and personal priorities.

The goal is simple: **help people understand what they're buying, what they're compromising on, and which option best suits their needs.**

> Built for the Hacktoberfest 2026 DEV Challenge — Week 2: *Side Hustle*.

## 💡 The Problem

Choosing the right product online can be surprisingly difficult. Shoppers often face:

- Too many products with confusing specifications.
- Difficulty comparing features that matter to them personally.
- Uncertainty about whether a cheaper option offers sufficient value.
- Reviews and recommendations that don't reflect their specific needs.
- Limited clarity about the trade-offs between competing products.

WorthIt? aims to make these decisions simpler, clearer, and more personalized.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Personalized Product Comparison** — Compare products according to individual needs and priorities.
- **Budget-Aware Recommendations** — Explore options within a specified budget.
- **Simple Specification Explanations** — Understand technical features without needing specialist knowledge.
- **Transparent Value Analysis** — Examine advantages, disadvantages, and trade-offs rather than relying on a single score.
- **Source-Backed Product Information** — Display product details, prices, and their sources wherever verified information is available.
- **AI-Assisted Decision Support** — Use an open-weight language model to interpret preferences and explain comparisons.

*These features are planned and will be implemented incrementally.*

## 🎯 Initial Scope

The first version will focus on **wireless earphones**.

Users will be able to describe their requirements—for example:

> “I need wireless earphones under ₹1,500 for online classes. Microphone quality and comfort matter more to me than bass.”

WorthIt? will use available product data to compare suitable options and explain how well each matches those priorities.

The initial prototype will use a small, verified product dataset. Live shopping integrations and additional product categories may be explored later.

## 🧠 Why Open-Source AI?

WorthIt? is intended to use an open-weight AI model for interpreting user preferences and generating understandable explanations.

The project will combine AI with structured product data and deterministic comparison logic. Product specifications, prices, and calculations should be grounded in verifiable information rather than generated guesses.

The long-term objective is to explore a more transparent and adaptable alternative to generic AI shopping recommendations.

WorthIt? is intended to support purchasing decisions, not replace independent judgment. Recommendations will depend on the quality, completeness, and freshness of the available product data. Prices and availability may change, and users should verify important details with the original seller.
